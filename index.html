Questions buyers ask us every week

Straight answers, the same ones you'd get on the phone.

Does it cost me anything to work with you?

The call is completely free, and in most cases so is our buyer representation. Our compensation is typically handled within the transaction, and we'll explain exactly how it works, in plain English, before you commit to anything.

Do I need to be pre-approved before booking a call?

No. The call is a great place to start if you haven't talked to a lender yet. We'll connect you with trusted local lenders and show you how pre-approval actually strengthens your offer.

I'm 3–6 months out. Is it too early to talk?

It's the ideal time. The buyers who win in this market set their strategy, budget, and financing before they start touring. When the right home shows up, you'll be ready to move faster than everyone else.

Isn't it a bad time to buy right now?

The right time to buy is when it's right for your life and your finances, and there are real opportunities in every market. On the call we'll look at what's actually happening in your price range and area, so you decide with data instead of headlines.

How can I compete without offering more money?

Price is only one part of an offer. Sellers also care about certainty and convenience: terms, timelines, deposit, and contingencies. That's exactly what we'll walk you through on the call.

I'm relocating from out of state. Can you help remotely?

Yes, we do this all the time. We shoot detailed video walkthroughs of every home, handle negotiations, and manage the process end to end. Buyers have purchased with us from 3,000 miles away.

What happens on the call? Is it a sales pitch?

No pitch. It's 15–20 minutes about your goals, your timeline, and your market, and you'll leave with a clear next step whether or not we work together. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you.

Olympia, Lacey, Tumwater, Yelm, Tacoma, Lakewood, Puyallup, Shelton, Gig Harbor, and the wider Thurston, Pierce, Mason, Kitsap, and Lewis County area.
